# Climate Guitar Cabinet — Final Measurements (woodworker handoff)

Two-compartment box: **sealed vault on top** (holds a floor guitar rack + guitars)
over an **open, vented amp compartment** below. The front is open for a glass door
(made separately).

> **The INNER (clear cavity) dimensions are the requirement — build to hit these.**
> Outer dimensions assume **19 mm (¾ in) carcass** and a **13 mm (½ in) back**.
> If you use different thicknesses, keep the inner cavities and adjust the outer.

## Material thickness
- Sides, top, bottom, middle divider: **19 mm (¾ in)**
- Back panel: **13 mm (½ in)**

## OUTER (overall carcass)
| | cm | inches |
|---|---|---|
| Width | **100.8** | 39.7 |
| Depth (carcass) | **44.3** | 17.4 |
| Height (carcass) | **185.7** | 73.1 |

The door adds ~2 cm at the front. With an optional base/plinth (~14 cm) the total
height is ~200 cm.

## INNER (clear cavities — the spec)
| Compartment | Width | Depth | Height |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vault** (top, sealed) | **97 cm / 38.2 in** | **43 cm / 16.9 in** | **125 cm / 49.2 in** |
| **Amp zone** (bottom, open) | **97 cm / 38.2 in** | **43 cm / 16.9 in** | **55 cm / 21.7 in** |

- **Divider:** top face **56.9 cm (22.4 in)** from the floor (= 19 mm bottom + 55 cm amp zone).
- **Cable hole:** Ø **5 cm (2 in)** in the divider, centered across the width, **6 cm (2.5 in)** from the back edge.

Top/bottom/divider are housed **between** the two full-height sides; the back overlays
the rear. No internal hanger board (guitars sit in a floor rack).

## Why these numbers (sized to the real loadout)
- **Width 97 cm inner** — fits the two Orange combos side by side (~90 cm) with clearance; the guitar rack (~82 cm) fits easily.
- **Depth 43 cm inner** — clears the amps (~27 cm) and the floor rack (~37 cm) with the guitars leaning, with door clearance.
- **Vault 125 cm inner** — the longest guitar (~45 in bass) standing in the rack is ~118 cm tall; ~7 cm headroom.
- **Amp zone 55 cm inner** — clears the taller Orange combo (~48.5 cm) with ~6 cm vent gap above.

## Notes
- Glass door + cherry face frame are separate; the **vault door opening** is ~97 × 125 cm.
- All **vault** joints airtight (glue + caulk); the **amp zone stays open/vented**.
- If you ever drop the amps, the box can shrink further (no 55 cm amp zone, and width can come down toward ~85 cm for just the rack).
