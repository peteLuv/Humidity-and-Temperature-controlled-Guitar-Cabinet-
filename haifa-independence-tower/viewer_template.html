How the shape was derived

The sketches set the shape; the area schedule sets every dimension. A schematic plan traced off the site drawing is scaled once so the ground floor nets its tabled area, then each upper slab is that outline scaled about the tight south-east tip until it hits its own tabled area.

That single rule reproduces what the section shows: a podium at ground and level 1, a waist at level 2, and a steadily raking north-west facade all the way to level 21. Every one of the 27 levels lands on its target area to within 0.05 m².

Fixed inputs

  • 5 basements at 2.80 m, 3,200 m² each
  • Ground and level 1 at 5.00 m
  • Levels 2–21 at m
  • Patio open from grade through the roof, constant in plan

Read with care

  • Plan proportions and the plot bearing (°) are scaled off the sketches, not surveyed.
  • Basements are shown as one plate enlarged about the tower centroid; in reality they would run asymmetrically under the plaza.
  • The grey tower and its podium are schematic context only.
