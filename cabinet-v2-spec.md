# Climate Cabinet — V2 (compact)

Same design as V1 (sealed cedar vault over an open, vented amp zone; cherry frame;
tempered-glass door), **shorter and shallower** to fit a tighter envelope.

**Envelope target:** ≤ 114 cm wide · ≤ 47 cm deep · ≤ 200 cm tall.

## External dimensions

| | **V2 (compact)** | V1 |
|---|---|---|
| Width | **40 in / 101.6 cm** | 40 in / 101.6 cm |
| Depth | **18.5 in / 47.0 cm** | 23 in / 58.4 cm |
| Height | **78.5 in / 199.4 cm** | 85 in / 215.9 cm |
| Vault (sealed, interior) | **38.5 × 18 × ~52.25 in** | 38.5 × 21.5 × ~58.75 in |
| Amp zone (open, interior) | 38.5 × 18 × 24 in | 38.5 × 21.5 × 24 in |
| Divider top (from floor) | 25.5 in | 25.5 in |

Zone stack (floor → top): ¾″ bottom + **24″ amp zone** + ¾″ divider + **52.25″ vault** + ¾″ top = **78.5″**.

> Width kept at 40″ (you have room up to 44″/114 cm if you want more hanging
> width to offset the shorter/shallower body — just say so).

## What stays the same as V1
Same sheet/board count (**2× ¾″ + 1× ½″ Baltic birch, ~4 cherry boards**) — just shorter/shallower cuts, so material cost is **≈ V1 or a touch less**. Same climate system (AC Infinity Controller 79, cool-mist humidifier + mini dehumidifier, two fans), same base/plinth + levelers + anti-tip, same finishing scheme.

## Things to watch at the smaller size
- **Tight for the longest instrument.** The 45″ bass in a 52.25″ vault, hung near the top, lands ~2–3″ above the divider — it fits, but confirm against your actual longest instrument before cutting.
- **18″ interior depth** still clears the amps (~10.8″ deep) and face-out / edge-out hanging, but the pedalboard sits a little further back. Verify your widest instrument body depth.
- **Glazing is smaller** (~37 × 46), so a bit cheaper than V1's ~$384.
